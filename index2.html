Jeremiah 1:5

APPRECIATING GOD FOR MAKING ME HIS CHILD AND FOR FINDING ME WORTHY TO BE AN INSTRUMENT FOR HIS SERVICE. Jer 1:5.

What shall I render unto the Lord,? nothing but to say, THANK YOU LORD.

I've been shading uncontrollable tears of joy since your tributes started trooping in. Am so so, overwhelmed by these accolades from all of you.

I can't even remember being nice to anybody, but can vividly remember SERVING GOD wholeheartedly with EVERYTHING in me. And that, I'll continue till my days on Earth are over.

God brought most of my lost sons and daughters whom I called, "Children of Consolation" after over 46years, and 30 years of parting. Viz: Old Students of BCGSS, Gokana, Circle of Ministers and Foundation members of NUAS, Ajalli. What an amazing opportunity to be alive and to see them come together once again. Glory to God.

My REUNION with THESE three sets of people was what gave rise to this INNOVATION of writing tributes at my birthday. I've been celebrating my birthdays in the past, but THIS one is so unique. I am so delighted about it.

Permit me to stop here because I may be tempted to write ten more pages of speech if I am allowed to express how I feel from the bottom of my heart.

To my lovely husband, the Odogwu of my life, who did not only train me in part of my secondary School, but contributed immensely to assisting to create a conducive atmosphere for me to relate with ALL those people that passed through Paradise family to achieve the success we're celebrating today.

To All my lovely children, both Biological and Fosters, relatives, Ministerial Colleagues, Brethren in the Faith, friends, well wishers and Every Attendee here, I say a million thanks for believing in me. I couldn't have made any single impact in life without you.

Lord Jesus, I thank you for saving and calling me into your kingdom service, and pledge allegiance to serve you more faithfully till the end. I give ALL Glory to My Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, who paid the ultimate Sacrifice for my sins through His blood of Redemption.

Adaigbo Chinyere Onuogu

Today, we celebrate the goodness and faithfulness of Almighty God in the life of His beloved daughter, an exceptional woman of God, a mother, mentor, philanthropist and a shining example of Christian love and benevolence. MY PERSONAL ENCOUNTERS WITH MUMMY PARADISE:

My first encounter with you remains unforgettable. My late cousin, Pastor Temple Uka Nwankwo, drove me to your home in Ajalli. Along the way, he spoke so highly of your kindness, generosity, charitable disposition, mentorship and deep religious commitment. Upon our arrival at the Onuogu family compound, we received an extraordinary welcome, accompanied by generous Christmas gifts to take home.

My second encounter was during the burial of my beloved cousin, Late Pastor Temple Uka Nwankwo. Mummy, you practically took 100% responsibility for the burial arrangements and activities, ensuring that everything was properly organised and that his final journey was conducted with dignity, love and Christian honour.

My third memorable encounter was 21 years ago, when my first son was just three months old. You blessed him and assured us of your willingness to support the education of all my children to whatever level they desired, out of your love and respect for my late cousin, Temple. Mummy, your prayers, blessings, generosity and goodwill have continued to follow my children. Today, by God's grace, they are all studying in universities in the United States of America. This is a testimony I will never forget.

YOU HAVE BECOME A MOTHER TO MY ENTIRE FAMILY. Since those encounters, you have remained a true mother to me and my family, and you will continue to be one forever. Your doors have always been open, your heart has remained generous, and whenever I have approached you for assistance, you have responded with remarkable kindness and promptness.

Mummy, some people preach the gospel with words, but you demonstrate it through practical love, sacrifice, generosity and service to humanity. Your life reminds us that true greatness is not measured by wealth or titles but by the number of lives touched, encouraged, supported and transformed.
