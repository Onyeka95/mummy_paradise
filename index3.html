MESSAGE FROM THE CELEBRANT
Uncontrollable Tears of Joy
APPRECIATING GOD FOR MAKING ME HIS CHILD AND FOR FINDING ME WORTHY TO BE AN INSTRUMENT FOR HIS SERVICE (JEREMIAH 1:5).

What shall I render unto the Lord? Nothing but to say, THANK YOU LORD.

I have been shedding uncontrollable tears of joy since your tributes started trooping in. I am so, so overwhelmed by these accolades from all of you. I can't even remember being nice to anybody, but I vividly remember SERVING GOD wholeheartedly with EVERYTHING in me. And that, I will continue till my days on Earth are over.

God brought most of my lost sons and daughters whom I called, "Children of Consolation" after over 46 years, and 30 years of partings. Viz: Old Students of BCGSS, Gokana, Circle of Ministers and Foundation members of NUAS, Ajalli. What an amazing opportunity to be alive and to see them come together once again. Glory to God!

My REUNION with THESE three sets of people was what gave rise to this INNOVATION of writing tributes at my birthday. I've been celebrating my birthdays in the past, but THIS one is so unique. I am so delighted about it. Permit me to stop here because I may be tempted to write ten more pages of speech if I am allowed to express how I feel from the bottom of my heart.

To my lovely husband, the Odogwu of my life, who did not only train me in part of my secondary school, but contributed immensely to assisting to create a conducive atmosphere for me to relate with ALL those people that passed through Paradise family to achieve the success we're celebrating today.

To all my lovely children, both Biological and Fosters, relatives, Ministerial Colleagues, Brethren in the Faith, friends, well-wishers and Every Attendee here, I say a million thanks for believing in me. I couldn't have made any single impact in life without you. Lord Jesus, I thank you for saving and calling me into your kingdom service, and pledge allegiance to serve you more faithfully till the end. I give ALL Glory to My Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, who paid the ultimate Sacrifice for my sins through His blood of Redemption.

Adaigbo Chinyere Onuogu God's Special Servant in His Vineyard
