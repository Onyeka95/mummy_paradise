CELEBRATING a Woman of Valour, Iconic, Charismatic, Patriotic and Enigmatic Chinyere Onuogu (Mummy Paradise)

On one of those quiet evenings during the Christmas season, l saw joyful, cheerful & unassuming woman of purpose drove into my compound at Umueve Ajalli and she came all the way to see Pastor Walter Okereke my very senior brother who in service to God and humanity chose to stay back in Ajalli to teach at Community Secondary School while evangelizing the town through Ajalli Christian Union.

This woman of valour & purpose is Mrs. Chinyere Onuogu who came with unmeasured humility & unparalleled commitment to discuss with Pastor Walter Okereke how Ajalli Christian Union Christmas crusade at Government field Ajalli was going to be organized that year and her charismatic plan to give gifts, minister to Widows in Ajalli by distributing food stuffs, rice, wrappers & money to widows who hitherto looked up to many without help.

For years, Mrs. Chinyere Onuogu has continued to support the yearly crusades organized by Ajalli Christian Union and has never failed to faithfully minister to Widows in the community by consistent distribution of foods stuff, bags of rice, wrappers, clothes and sharing of God's word of grace & salvation by which today, many widows in Ajalli have been drawn to Christ as professing Christians.

Upon my election as National President of Ajalli Students Union I had close interaction with then President-General (PG) of Ajalli Welfare Union (AWU) late Chief, Engr, Dr. Patrick Okoli Onwumere who in one of our meetings with him around 1995 commended efforts of Mummy Paradise in seeking support of Ajalli Welfare Union for effective organization of National Union of Ajalli Students (NUA5) and mentioned to me that this Woman of God has been preaching to him and other members of Ajalli Welfare Union (AWU) to repent & give their lives to Christ and has requested that Ajalli Welfare Union should be in forefront of organization of Prayers for the land and crusades by Ajalli Christian Union (ACU).

Eventually before he passed on late Engr Patrick Onwumere became strong Christian sponsoring free distribution of Rhapsody of Realities published by Christ Embassy in Ajalli and definitely years of ministration of word of God to him and many others by Mummy Paradise culminated in his salvation and service to Christ. Indeed words will fail me to write more on Chinyere Onuogu support to National Union of Ajalli Students & Ajalli as a whole.

A Tribute to Our Mummy Paradise - Pastor Mrs. Onuogu at 66

Mummy Paradise,

On this special occasion of your 66th birthday, I rise to celebrate a woman whose life has been a living epistle of love, sacrifice, and faithfulness.

Our paths first crossed in the late 1990s, in the quiet town of Ajalli, Anambra State. We were just young ministers, full of fire but with very little in our hands. You saw us, not as strangers, but as your own children in the Lord. You opened your heart, your home, and your hands to us.

You supported our outreaches. You gave financially, and you gave even more in ways money cannot buy - your counsel, your prayers, your encouragement, your presence, and your food on days when we had nothing. You believed in the vision God gave to us even when we ourselves were still struggling to understand it.

We will not forget that life happened. Hurts came amongst us, and the group could not stand together as we once hoped. Many would have walked away at that point. But not you, Mummy. You refused to accept the demise of the vision. You kept calling, kept checking on the ones you could reach, kept praying, and kept trying to bring us together, reminding us of why God brought us together in the first place. That is the mark of a true mother in the faith - one who does not abandon her children when things become difficult.

Today, as we look back, we can remember the droplets of love that have played a major part in much of who we have become as pulpit ministers and apostles in the market place today. You watered the seed in us at Ajalli. You contributed to our growing up, not just in ministry, but in life, in character, and in our walk with God.

As you mark 66 years, we pray that the Lord who has kept you will reward you openly. May your strength be renewed like the eagle's. May your latter years be more glorious than your former. May you reap in joy all that you have sown in tears, in prayers, and in giving. May your children, both biological and spiritual, rise and call you blessed.

Though I will not be able to be physically present to celebrate with you, please know that my heart is there with you, full of gratitude and honour. You are loved, you are appreciated, and you will never be forgotten.

Happy 66th Birthday, Mummy Paradise!
Thank you for being our mother, our supporter, our intercessor, and our bridge-builder.

With deep love and honour,

Joyce
