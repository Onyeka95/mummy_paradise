HAPPY 66TH BIRTHDAY TRIBUTE!

To: Amb. Dr. Mrs. Chinyere Onuogu (Mummy Paradise)

CELEBRATING 66 YEARS OF GOD'S FAITHFULNESS, LOVE, CHARITY AND SELFLESS SERVICE TO HUMANITY.

Today, we celebrate the goodness and faithfulness of Almighty God in the life of His beloved daughter, an exceptional woman of God, a mother, mentor, philanthropist and a shining example of Christian love and benevolence.

As you clock 66, Mummy, we celebrate not just another year of life but a remarkable journey of faith, compassion and unwavering commitment to God's ministry of charity, particularly towards orphans, vulnerable youths and the elderly.

MY PERSONAL ENCOUNTERS WITH MUMMY PARADISE

My first encounter with you remains unforgettable. My late cousin, Pastor Temple Uka Nwankwo, drove me to your home in Ajalli. Along the way, he spoke so highly of your kindness, generosity, charitable disposition, mentorship and deep religious commitment.

Upon our arrival at the Onuogu family compound, we received an extraordinary welcome, accompanied by generous Christmas gifts to take home. That experience gave me a first-hand understanding of the wonderful woman my cousin had always spoken about.

My second encounter was during the burial of my beloved cousin, Late Pastor Temple Uka Nwankwo. Mummy, you practically took 100% responsibility for the burial arrangements and activities, ensuring that everything was properly organised and that his final journey was conducted with dignity, love and Christian honour. Your prayers and comforting words after the burial brought consolation to us during that difficult period.

My third memorable encounter was 21 years ago, when my first son was just three months old. You blessed him and assured us of your willingness to support the education of all my children to whatever level they desired, out of your love and respect for my late cousin, Temple.

Mummy, your prayers, blessings, generosity and goodwill have continued to follow my children. Today, by God's grace, they are all studying in universities in the United States of America. This is a testimony I will never forget.

YOU HAVE BECOME A MOTHER TO MY ENTIRE FAMILY.

Since those encounters, you have remained a true mother to me and my family, and you will continue to be one forever. Your doors have always been open, your heart has remained generous, and whenever I have approached you for assistance, you have responded with remarkable kindness and promptness.

Mummy, some people preach the gospel with words, but you demonstrate it through practical love, sacrifice, generosity and service to humanity.

Your life reminds us that true greatness is not measured by wealth or titles but by the number of lives touched, encouraged, supported and transformed.

MY BIRTHDAY PRAYERS FOR YOU

As you celebrate your 66th birthday, may Almighty God:

  • Renew your strength, health and youthful spirit.
  • Continue to enlarge your capacity to serve humanity.
  • Reward every seed of kindness you have sown.
  • Grant you many more fruitful years of joy, peace and prosperity.
  • Preserve your family and generations after you.
  • Give you greater grace to fulfil your divine assignment on earth.

May your good works continue to speak for you on earth, and may your heavenly reward be everlasting.

HAPPY 66TH BIRTHDAY, MUMMY PARADISE!

God bless you abundantly, Mummy, both here on earth and in eternity.

May we all learn to emulate your uncommon love for humanity, your generosity and your unwavering commitment to God's work.

With deep love, gratitude and prayers,

Hon. Gabriel Nwankwo

HAPPY 66TH BIRTHDAY, MUMMY CHINYERE ONUOGU!

66 years of God's faithfulness, grace and divine impact

Today, we celebrate a remarkable woman, a mother of virtue, a woman of grace, strength, wisdom and uncommon blessings. Mummy Chinyere Onuogu, your 66th birthday is not just a celebration of age but a glorious testimony of God's faithfulness, preservation and abundant goodness.

For 66 wonderful years, God has been your strength, your keeper and your unfailing source of help. Through the different seasons of life, His mercy has sustained you, His grace has carried you, and His faithfulness has remained constant.

You are a blessing to your generation, a source of encouragement to many, and a woman whose life reminds us that age is not merely a number but a testimony of God's goodness.

At 66, you are not growing weaker; you are growing stronger in grace, deeper in wisdom, richer in experience and greater in divine relevance. Your best days are not behind you; by the special grace of God, greater testimonies, greater celebrations and greater accomplishments are still ahead.

As you celebrate this beautiful milestone, my prayer is that the Almighty God will renew your strength like that of the eagle. May He bless you with sound health, long life, divine protection, overflowing joy and uncommon peace.

May your children, family and loved ones continue to surround you with happiness. May you eat the fruits of your labour, enjoy the rewards of your sacrifices and witness generations after generations flourishing under God's blessings.

May the remaining years of your life be more glorious than the years behind you. May your testimony never become history, and may your latter days be filled with greater honour, laughter and supernatural celebrations.

Happy 66th Birthday, Mummy Chinyere Onuogu!

You are celebrated, cherished, honoured and deeply appreciated.

Congratulations on 66 glorious years! Your life shall continue to shine brighter and brighter unto the perfect day, in Jesus' mighty name. Amen!

Scriptural Anchor: Psalm 92:12–14; Isaiah 46:4; Proverbs 16:31.

With love and prayers.

Happy 66th Birthday, Mummy Chinyere! May heaven continue to celebrate you, and may your testimony inspire many generations.

Yours In Him.

Pst Maxwell Ifeanyi Anozie

HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO A REMARKABLE WOMAN!

Today, I celebrate not just another year of your life but a woman whose kindness, generosity, love, and support have made a great difference in my life.

You are more than a mother to us; you are a blessing, a source of encouragement, and a woman whose good deeds I will never take for granted.

Your willingness to support us, stand by us, and contribute to all the crusades we had in Ageli in Anambra State, is something I deeply appreciate.

Your generosity has touched our lives in ways you may never fully understand.

You have showed us that true greatness is not just about what we achieve but also about how much we give and how many lives we touch.

On this special day, I pray that Almighty God will continue to bless you abundantly, grant you good health, long life, peace, happiness, and unlimited favour. May He reward your kindness, enlarge your coast, and open greater doors of opportunities for you.

May your new age bring you countless reasons to smile, celebrate, and testify to God's goodness.

Happy Birthday to my wonderful mummy, a woman of substance, grace, and uncommon generosity.

May you continue to shine and flourish!

With love and deep appreciation.

Deaconess Comfort Adelaja
